PIXELZ / BUYER INFORMATION

HONEST INFO.
BEFORE ORDERS.

This page is a pre-launch notice and compliance checklist, not legal advice or a finished trading policy. PIXELZ is not accepting orders or payments through this preview.

IMPORTANT

We cannot certify legal compliance for a business that has not yet selected its legal seller and supplier. A parent/guardian should help confirm the exact requirements with Egyptian authorities and a qualified local professional before trading begins.

1. Current status

Product cards are category concepts and sourcing research, not confirmed stock or a live offer for sale. The bag is a shortlisting tool only. No checkout, payment capture, order form, customer account, contact collection or mailing list is active.

2. Product price and description

Before any item is advertised for sale, PIXELZ should show the exact item/variant, specifications, compatibility, seller-confirmed stock, full price and delivery fees, expected delivery date, applicable warranty, and reviewed exchange/return steps. Competitor prices on the supplier page are market comparisons, not PIXELZ prices.

3. Consumer protection, returns and defects

The Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency states a general 14-day return/exchange right after receipt, subject to legal exceptions, and a 30-day period for defective goods. The exact item and facts matter; the final store policy must be checked so it does not promise less than the law requires. See the official CPA FAQ ↗ and return/exchange guidance ↗.

4. Seller identity and invoice

The CPA says an invoice should include the transaction date, product price and product specifications/nature/type/quantity. Before launching, seller identity, required registration/tax details, contact information, delivery terms, warranty information and other details required for the specific transaction need to be confirmed and shown. We have not invented a business number, legal name or address for this preview. See the CPA's specific invoice-data guidance ↗ and its definitions page ↗.

5. Tax and registration

The Egyptian Tax Authority says e-commerce tax treatment depends on applicable income-tax and VAT rules, with a simplified system under Law 6/2025 for eligible enrollees; there isn't a single separate tax that replaces the other obligations. The exact requirements depend on the actual seller and activity. Check directly with the Egyptian Tax Authority's e-commerce guidance ↗ and MSMEDA support ↗. Because the founders are under 18, a parent/guardian should handle legal seller status, registration, tax questions, supplier contracts and merchant-account eligibility.

6. Personal data

This static preview doesn't collect customer details. Before adding checkout, WhatsApp or enquiry forms, PIXELZ needs an accurate privacy notice and a reviewed data-handling process covering purpose, minimisation, retention, security, customer rights, processors and any applicable cross-border/registration obligations. Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020 and its later executive regulations require up-to-date local review. Official starting point: Government-hosted text of Personal Data Protection Law 151/2020 ↗. For current executive-regulation interpretation, see this dated EIPR review (28 July 2026) ↗; it is analysis, not the law itself.

7. Launch checklist

01 / LEGAL SELLER

Adult seller, applicable business/tax registrations, contact address and seller details verified.

02 / SUPPLIER TESTED

Written resale/dropship terms, exact stock, sample quality, delivery and returns confirmed.

03 / HONEST LISTINGS

Actual product photos, exact specs, total price, delivery estimate and compatibility.

04 / BUYER RIGHTS

Reviewed invoice, returns, exchanges, defects and warranty process.

05 / PRIVACY

Reviewed notice, minimum data collection, secure handling and deletion process.

06 / PAYMENT READY

Approved adult-owned merchant account, secure gateway setup and refund testing.

8. Support contact

PIXELZ hasn't published a seller address or customer-support contact because the legal seller and fulfilment arrangements have not been finalised. Those details must be real and verified before accepting orders.

Important: This is a practical pre-launch checklist, not a legal opinion or a guarantee of compliance. Confirm the specific business model with the relevant Egyptian authorities and a qualified local adviser before enabling paid sales.
