VERIFY FIRST.

We compared local and international routes for gaming setup accessories. No supplier listed here has passed a sample order or a written fulfilment check with PIXELZ yet.

NOT APPROVED YET

Before accepting money, confirm the real SKU and stock, resale permission, supplier cost, shipping to the customer, COD/payout terms, warranty, returns and an actual sample. A platform that supports dropshipping is not automatically a trustworthy supplier.

01

LOCAL CANDIDATE TO CONTACT FIRST

YallaBuy

BEST LOCAL LEAD TO VERIFY

YallaBuy's own About page describes Egyptian distribution, gaming/tech accessories and brands such as AULA, Onikuma, Cougar and Dobe. Its warranty page lists 12-month warranty coverage for some named brands/products. These are the company's own public statements; they do not establish that it will dropship to our customer or offer wholesale terms to PIXELZ.

ASK FOR WRITTEN CONFIRMATION

Reseller pricing and permission; direct dispatch for individual orders; neutral or branded packaging; current stock file; invoice requirements; exact warranty provider; return address; delivery costs and timing by governorate; failed-delivery/COD settlement terms.

Decision: contact first and request a sample/quote. Do not call them a PIXELZ partner until the terms and sample are verified.

02

LOCAL DROPSHIPPING PLATFORM TO TEST

Drop-X

PLATFORM, NOT THE ITEM SUPPLIER

Drop-X's public pricing page advertises a free tier for up to 10 products and mentions COD. It also advertises paid tiers. Those details can change, and we have not verified which products exist in its catalogue or what fees and payout rules apply to a real order.

TEST BEFORE COMMITTING

Search the exact categories on the store; identify the underlying seller; confirm landed cost, COD fees, payout delay, returns, subscription conditions and whether products are actually available. Ask for a sample before launch.

FIRST PRODUCT TESTS

SIMPLE ITEMS FIRST.

01 / DESK MATS

Verify size, fabric, stitched edge, real photos, packed dimensions and stock.

02 / CABLE TIDY

Check quantity, adhesive, cable-size fit and residue risk.

03 / HEADSET STANDS

Test stability, finish, package size and damage in transit.

04 / KEYBOARD ADD-ONS

Check layout, keycap profile, stem and total key count.

05 / CONTROLLER GRIPS

Name compatible controller models and verify the exact contents.

06 / LIGHTING

Check power/plug, heat, controls, safety details and returns.
